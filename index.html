Moving around

  • Orbit: drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan.
  • Top: straight down, like a dollhouse. Turn the roof off to see inside.
  • Walk: click the view, drag to look, WASD or arrows to move, Shift to hurry, Esc to leave.
  • Tour: a guided walk through every room, up and down the stairs. Space or the play button pauses, drag to look around on the way, any movement key takes over in Walk, Esc ends it. The Plan view shows the route while a tour is loaded.
  • Plan: the same house as a scaled 2D drawing with dimensions.
  • Floors: pick a storey to see it as a dollhouse with everything above removed, or All for the whole house. The Plan view follows the same choice.

Changing the house

Everything comes from one JSON spec. Open the Spec tab, change a number, press Apply. The walls, doors, roof, plan and areas are all derived from it.

  • rooms: rectangles in metres. x,y is the south-west corner, w runs east, d runs north. Rooms that touch share a wall automatically.
  • openings: type is door, window or opening. Place it on a room and a side (north, south, east, west). offset is measured from the west end of a north or south wall, and from the south end of an east or west wall.
  • Windows take sill and height. Doors swing into the room they are placed on.
  • roof: gable or flat, with pitch in degrees, overhang, and ridge set to x, y or auto.
  • wallHeight, exteriorWall, interiorWall: metres.
  • site.lake: a lake on the plot, placed with the same x, y, w, d convention as a room, plus an optional rotate in degrees. Remove it for a dry plot.
  • floors: a list of storeys, each with name, level (0 is the entrance floor, negative is below ground), height and its own rooms, openings, decks and stairs. Storeys stack automatically with a 0.3 m slab between them.
  • decks: terraces at that storey’s level, material wood (on posts down to the ground) or stone; a wooden deck well above the ground gets a railing on its free edges, or set railing to true or false. stairs: x, y, w, d, dir and shape straight or u, rising to the storey above.
  • site.terrain: ground height high north of highY and low south of lowY, sloping between, for a house dug into a hillside.
  • site.trees: a list of trees (x, y, h, kind leaf, pine or birch) and/or a scatter (count, seed, area) that plants trees at random but never on the house, decks or lake. Change the seed for another arrangement.
  • site.plot (boundary hedge, fence or none, with sides), site.paths (polyline points, width, gravel or stone), site.jetty, site.beds (flowers or shrubs) and site.neighbours (plain house blocks with h and a roof) dress the plot. Paths cut a gap through the boundary where they cross it.
  • furniture on a room: items with a type (bed, single-bed, sofa, armchair, chair, table, coffee-table, desk, nightstand, wardrobe, shelf, tv-bench, bench, kitchen, island, stove, fridge, appliance, toilet, basin, bathtub, shower, sauna-bench, or box with a label), a centre x, y in metres from the room's south-west corner, optional w, d, h and rot (0, 90, 180, 270; the back of beds, sofas and chairs points to the item's own south before turning). The Furniture button hides them all.
  • tour: the route for the Tour button, as stops, each a room name or { "level": 1, "room": "Kök", "pause": 2 }, or x,y for a spot outside. A stop without level stays on the storey of the one before. look (a room name or {x, y}) turns the view during a pause, speed is metres per second. The tour finds its own way through the doors and up the stairs. Leave tour out and it visits every room.

Working with Claude Code

Say what you want changed. Claude edits house.json, runs node build.mjs and republishes this page. If you tweaked the spec here, press Copy JSON and paste it into the chat so the file catches up.
